## The Problem

### The Ayurvedic Retail Market is Massive — and Massively Underserved

India's Ayurvedic market is valued at **$18 billion** (2024) and projected to reach **$30+ billion by 2030**, growing at 15%+ CAGR. Nepal's herbal medicine market follows similar trajectories. Yet:

- **90% of Ayurvedic stores** still operate on paper-based systems or basic billing software
- **Batch tracking and expiry management** — legally required for medicines — is done manually, leading to expired product sales and regulatory risk
- **No existing POS system** understands Ayurvedic-specific needs: batch/expiry tracking for herbal medicines, multi-language product names (Sanskrit, Hindi, Nepali), GST/VAT compliance across India and Nepal
- **Connectivity is unreliable** in the tier-2/3 cities and rural areas where most Ayurvedic stores operate — cloud-only solutions fail
- **Customer retention** is purely relationship-driven with zero data-backed loyalty programs

### What Happens Without RishiPath?

| Pain Point | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| No batch/expiry tracking | Expired medicines reach customers → health risk, legal liability, revenue loss |
| Paper-based inventory | 15-25% inventory shrinkage due to theft, damage, and miscounting |
| No customer data | Zero retention strategy → customers lost to competitors |
| Manual supplier management | Late payments, strained relationships, stock-outs |
| No business analytics | Decisions made on gut feeling, not data |
| Internet dependency | Lost sales during outages (common in rural India/Nepal) |

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Built for Ayurvedic, Not Adapted

Unlike generic POS systems, RishiPath understands:
- **Medicine batch tracking** with expiry dates (color-coded: safe → warning → expired)
- **FIFO inventory** — oldest stock sells first, preventing expiry waste
- **Product types**: Choornas (powders), Tailams (oils), Ghritams (ghee), Capsules, Rasayanas
- **Multi-language names**: English + Sanskrit + Hindi + Nepali for every product
- **Prescription tracking** for regulated products
- **Shelf life management** with automatic expiry alerts

### 2. Offline-First — Works Everywhere

- **Full POS functionality** without internet — no lost sales during outages
- **Local database** on each POS machine
- **Automatic sync** when internet returns
- Perfect for **tier-2/3 cities and rural areas** where 60%+ of Ayurvedic stores operate

### 3. White-Label Multi-Tenant

- **One codebase, unlimited brands** — each organization gets its own identity
- **Cost-effective scaling** for franchise chains, ashram networks, and cooperative groups
- **Country-level customization**: India (GST, INR, Razorpay) vs Nepal (VAT, NPR, eSewa/Khalti)

### 4. Loyalty That Actually Works

- **4-Tier System**: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum
- **Automatic progression** — customers see real rewards as they shop more
- **Birthday bonuses**, reward redemption, tier-specific discounts
- **Data shows**: loyalty programs increase repeat purchases by **25-40%** in retail

### 5. Complete Supplier Management

- Full **purchase order lifecycle**: Draft → Ordered → Received → Paid
- **Supplier ledger** with real-time balance tracking
- **Purchase returns** with batch-level traceability
- **Payment allocation** — every rupee traced from supplier to shelf
